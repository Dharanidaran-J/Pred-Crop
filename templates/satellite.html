Mathematical Formulation

The Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) is mathematically defined as:

NDVI = (NIR - RED) / (NIR + RED)

Healthy chlorophyll in photosynthetic active leaf tissue absorbs visible Red light (Band 4: 665nm) while the mesophyll cellular structure strongly reflects Near-Infrared radiation (Band 8: 842nm).

Agricultural Index Scale

  • 0.70 – 1.00: Peak canopy, maximum leaf area index (LAI), high yield potential.
  • 0.50 – 0.69: Healthy vegetative growth, active tillering.
  • 0.30 – 0.49: Moderate greenness, early emergence or soil moisture deficit.
  • 0.15 – 0.29: Sparse germination, harvest stubble, or dry fallow.
  • < 0.15: Water bodies, paved infrastructure, or bare rock/soil.
